Trust and safety

Fees

Three small fees, 0.2% at most, each fixed in the contracts when they were deployed. Nobody can change them later, and none is ever taken from your salary, your vault or your collateral.

{{PLAIN}}

Buying once is free. A recurring buy costs 0.15% of what it buys: 15 cents on $100. A loan costs 0.1% a year on top of the lender's interest: about $1 on $1,000 borrowed for a year. Earning interest costs 0.1% a year, paid only out of the interest you earn.

Gifts, withdrawals and collecting your pay are free (you only pay the network's own small gas fee).

The three fees

FeeRateWhen it is takenWhere in the code
Recurring buy0.15%On the USDC a recurring plan puts into an asset: an every-payday plan or a recurring buy. One-time buys are free.Payroll, SipRouter
Borrow0.1% a yearBuilds up by the second on the amount owed, paid on repayment before the loan. You receive the full amount you borrow. If a loan is liquidated instead, the unpaid fee is dropped.EmployeeVault, set in VaultFactory
Earn0.1% a yearOn USDC earning interest, taken only out of the interest when you stop earning. If interest is lower, the fee is only the interest.EmployeeVault, set in VaultFactory

The borrow fee is shown inside one Borrow APY together with Morpho's interest, which goes to Morpho's lenders, not to Stackday. Every fee is visible on the screen (and on chat cards) before you confirm.

The rules every fee follows

  • Fixed at deployment. Fee rates are set when a contract is deployed, and no function exists to change them.
  • Capped in code at 0.2% (20 basis points). A deployment with a higher fee would fail.
  • Never from salary paid out, never from money in a vault, never from collateral.
  • No fee on gifts. The GiftBox contract has no fee setting at all.
  • The fee wallet has no powers. It only receives.
