Reference

Glossary

The words used in Stackday and in these docs, in plain language.

TermMeaning
BaseA blockchain network built by Coinbase. Stackday is built on it.
Base SepoliaBase's test network. Same technology, but the money has no value. Chain id 84532.
WalletAn app that holds your key and signs on your behalf. Whoever has the key controls the wallet.
SignatureYour wallet's approval of a message or transaction. Signing in costs nothing; transactions cost a small network fee (gas).
GasThe small fee the network charges to process a transaction, paid in ETH.
USDCA digital dollar issued by Circle. Stackday uses Circle's test USDC on Base Sepolia.
Tokenized stockA token that tracks a real share, such as Coinbase's AAPLc for Apple. For eligible people outside the US, with no voting rights.
Mock tokenA test stand-in for a real token, with a price the demo wallet controls. Names start with "m", such as mAAPL.
Smart contractA program on the blockchain that holds money and follows fixed rules.
VaultYour own smart contract that holds your assets. Only your wallet can take things out.
Free / lockedFree assets sit in your vault. Locked assets back a loan in a Morpho market.
SIPSystematic investment plan: buying a fixed amount on a schedule. In the code, a payday share is a "SIP".
Payday planA standing choice to invest a share of every payday into chosen assets.
Recurring buyA plan that buys an asset from your wallet on a schedule.
TreasuryThe employer's USDC balance inside the Payroll contract.
ScheduleA repeating payroll: daily, weekly, every 2 weeks or monthly.
Trigger bot (keeper)A small automated job that presses due buttons on chain each weekday, paying only gas.
MorphoAn existing lending protocol on Base. Stackday borrows through it and runs no lending pool of its own.
CollateralAssets you lock as a guarantee for a loan.
Liquidation limit (LLTV)The share of the collateral's value that can be borrowed before liquidation, for example 62.5% for Apple.
HealthYour limit divided by what you owe. Above 1.00 is safe; at 1.00 the loan can be liquidated.
LiquidationWhen a loan's health falls below 1.00, others may repay part of it and take collateral at a discount.
Safety capYour vault's own ceiling on borrowing, 50% of the limit by default, raised to 65% for High.
Emergency ruleAn optional rule that, when a payday is claimed while a loan is close to its limit, puts the payday share into collateral.
Collateral basketLocking several assets at once with one total and one limit, while each stays its own market.
Oracle, price feedA contract that reports a price. On testnet these are mocks.
SlippageHow much worse a trade's price can be than expected. Stackday refuses buys more than 1% worse.
bpsBasis points: hundredths of a percent. 15 bps = 0.15%.
APYAnnual percentage yield: the yearly rate including compounding.
AdapterA small contract that connects Stackday to an outside service (lending, prices, swaps, interest), so the service can be swapped.
Invest onlyAn asset you can buy and hold but not borrow against, because it has no Morpho market.
ThemeA topic such as AI and chips. Themes suggest a split of assets from fixed rules.
Weekly report, daily noteStackday's research: the Monday report for readers and the internal daily note used to build a track record.
ViewThe report's general stance on an asset: Accumulate, Hold, Reduce or Avoid new buys.
Fact (research)A number or item computed by code with a source and a time, which every claim must cite.
IntentThe chat model's structured request, such as "buy, Apple, 20 USDC", checked against a strict schema before code builds a card.
Action cardWhat the chat shows before you sign: the action, amounts, fee and your wallet as the receiver.
Pre-checkA simulation of a card's transactions on Base Sepolia before you sign.
Gift boxUSDC held by the GiftBox contract that buys a mix of assets into the recipient's vault when opened.
Stackday IDYour unique ID, such as SD-7K3F9Q2M, used to find you for teams and gifts.
Fork testA test that runs the code against a private copy of the real Base mainnet, with real tokens and markets, at no cost.
Private paySalary paid with encrypted amounts through Fairblock confidential transfers.
