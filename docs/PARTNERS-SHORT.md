# Stackday: tracks and partners, one page

Read on November 16, 2026. Full detail and sources: docs/PARTNERS-DETAILED.md.

## Recommended track

1. **Programmable Stock Markets ($20,000 Base pool). Enter here.** "Borrow against your stocks" is one of its four listed ideas and is exactly what we built and can show on testnet. The collateral basket and the Themes split also fit its "custom portfolios" and "build-your-own stock indexes" wording.
2. **Agentic Stock Finance ($20,000).** Only a fit if Themes becomes a real agent (research to portfolio, weekly rebalancing proposals). Not built yet.
3. **Meme and Community Economies ($10,000).** Poor fit. Every partner there pays for launching a coin.

**Can we enter more than one track?** The Official Rules say a project may enter more than one track only if the Quest Page allows it, and may win in only one. The Quest Page says nothing about it, so the default is **one track**. We should ask Base in their support group before assuming otherwise.

## Top 3 partner moves

1. **Bankr: apply, and do a light integration.** Its program has no token requirement on its page ($3,000 pool and $100 in AI credits on its page, $1,000 on the Base page). Partner winners are judged with the same criteria, and one project can win a track prize and a partner prize. Idea: a read-only "Stackday skill" so a Bankr chat agent can answer "how healthy is my loan, what did my last payday buy". About 1 day. Needs your approval (a chat channel is new).
2. **BaseStonk: skip integration, do not launch a token.** Its only reward is creator fees from a token you launch on it. That breaks our no-token rule.
3. **Stonks Exchange, o1 Exchange, Feel.cash, Hydropump: skip.** All four are coin launchpads (Feel.cash needs $300,000 in volume and a $100,000 market cap). Hydropump's page would not load.

## Main risks

- **US eligibility** (above). The biggest disqualification risk.
- **Token-launch pull.** Five of six partners reward launching a coin. Saying yes breaks the rule and pulls time from the judged path.
- **Wording.** No "investment advice", no voting rights or Coinbase/Base endorsement claims, say plainly that Stackday does not lend (Morpho does).
- **Scope creep.** Aave and an agent layer are tempting. The judged path must stay perfect first.
